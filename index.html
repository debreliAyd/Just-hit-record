FAQ

Every Question Answered

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need any filming experience?
+
None at all. We give you a simple shot list — what to film, how long each clip should be, and tips for good lighting. If you can hold a phone, you're qualified.
What does the $40/day ad spend cover?
+
The ~$40/day goes directly to Facebook/Meta to show your ads to local people matching your ideal customer. You pay Facebook directly — it's separate from our service fee.
How quickly will I see results?
+
Most clients see their first leads within 24–48 hours of launch. Results typically improve significantly from week 2 as we optimise the campaign.
Do I need a website?
+
No. We build a simple lead capture page as part of your setup — customers fill in their details and they come straight to you. No website required.
What countries do you work with?
+
We work fully remotely and currently serve businesses in Australia, the UK, New Zealand, the USA, Canada, and more. If Facebook Ads run in your country, we can help.
Are there long-term contracts?
+
No lock-in contracts. We work month-to-month. Our goal is results good enough that you'd never want to leave.
What businesses do you work with?
+
We specialise in local and service-based businesses — gyms, salons, trades, restaurants, clinics, real estate, and more. If you serve a local area, we can help.
What if I'm not happy with results?
+
We'll work with you until you are. We analyse what's not working, adjust the creative and targeting, and keep iterating. Your success is our business.

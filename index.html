FAQ

Every Question Answered

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need any filming experience?
+
None at all. We give you a simple shot list — what to film, how long each clip should be, and a few tips for good lighting. If you can hold a phone, you're qualified.
What does the $40/day ad spend cover?
+
The ~$40/day goes directly to Facebook/Meta to show your video ads to local people matching your ideal customer. You pay this straight to Facebook — it's separate from our service fee.
How quickly will I see results?
+
Most clients see their first leads within 24–48 hours of launch. The first week collects data — results typically improve significantly from week 2 as we optimise the campaign.
Do I need a website?
+
No. We build a simple lead capture page as part of your setup — customers fill in their details and they come straight to you. A website helps but is absolutely not required to get started.
What countries do you work with?
+
We work fully remotely and currently serve businesses in Australia, the UK, New Zealand, the USA, Canada, and more. If Facebook Ads run in your country, we can get you results.
Are there long-term contracts?
+
No lock-in contracts to start. We work month-to-month. Our goal is to deliver results good enough that you'd never want to leave — not to trap you with fine print.
What type of businesses do you work with?
+
We specialise in local and service-based businesses — gyms, salons, trades, restaurants, clinics, real estate, and more. If you serve a local area and want more customers, we can help.
What if I'm not happy with the results?
+
We'll work with you until you are. We analyse what's not working, adjust the creative, targeting, and offer, and keep iterating. We're invested in your results — if you win, we keep your business.
